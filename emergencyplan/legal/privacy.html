We do not ask for your name, your address, your phone number or your Eircode. We do not know where your house is. The answers you give about your household are kept with your email address and nothing else.

Where it is kept

On Supabase, in a database hosted in Ireland (AWS eu-west-1). Chosen on purpose: your data does not leave the European Union. Access is locked so the website can only add a record, never read one back.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until two years with no contact from you, whichever comes first. Then it goes, permanently.

Who else sees it

  • Supabase, as our database provider, in Ireland.
  • Vercel, which serves this page.
  • Meta, only if you accepted measurement on the bar at the bottom of the page, and only to tell us an advert worked. Decline and nothing is sent.
  • Digistore24, if you buy. They take the payment and they hold your payment details, not us. We never see your card.

We do not sell your data, we do not swap it and we do not hand it to data brokers.

Cookies

No advertising cookie is set unless you accept it on the bar at the bottom of the page. Decline and the site works exactly the same. We store one small value in your browser to remember your choice, and nothing else.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to stop using it, or take it elsewhere. One email does it, and we answer within thirty days. There is nothing to prove and no form to fill in.

If we get it wrong you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, dataprotection.ie, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28.

Email

You only get email beyond your result if you ticked the box. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works on the first click, with no login and no survey.
