What this book is, and what it is not

It is general household information for preparing an ordinary Irish home for power cuts, storms and floods. It is not professional advice.

  • Electrical work must be carried out by a Registered Electrical Contractor.
  • Questions about drinking water go to the HSE, or to the EPA for a private well.
  • Questions about medication go to your pharmacist or your GP.
  • Food safety guidance follows the FSAI and safefood.
  • In an emergency, ring 999 or 112.

The readiness check on this site gives an estimate from ten answers. It is not a survey of your house, it is not a measurement and it is not advice.
